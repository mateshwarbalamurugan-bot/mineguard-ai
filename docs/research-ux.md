# MINEGUARD AI — research and UX strategy

**Research reviewed:** 2026-11-04. Sources below are official vendor, standards or government pages. Vendor descriptions are attributed statements, not independent verification of performance. Interface patterns are reported only where the reviewed page documents them; design recommendations are labelled as inferences. This research does not establish MineGuard efficacy, accessibility conformance, certification, approval or integration compatibility.

## Research findings and design implications

### Sandvik Newtrax Mining Data Platform — mixed-fleet telemetry

**Official source:** [Sandvik Mining Data Platform / mixed-fleet telemetry](https://www.mining.sandvik/en/digital-solutions/operations-and-connected-fleet/mixed-fleet-telemetry/) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** Sandvik describes MDP as an OEM-agnostic platform for underground monitoring of people, equipment and environment, including positioning, 3D map, worker tag board, positioning tables, distress alerts, evacuation notifications, proximity analytics and environmental monitoring. The page also describes MineHop connectivity and equipment. **Documented interface pattern:** textual description of spatial view paired with tag board and positioning tables; no screenshots or interactions were verified. **MineGuard should learn:** show spatial context alongside a list/record view, group environmental/workforce/asset conditions, and expose data freshness and context. **Do differently:** clearly distinguish MineGuard simulation from vendor functions; do not imply integration, equivalence, continuous telemetry, validated controls or evacuation capability.

### Epiroc Mobilaris Mining Intelligence

**Official source:** [Epiroc Mobilaris Mining Intelligence](https://www.epiroc.com/en-ca/products/digital-solutions/safety-solutions/mobilaris-mining-intelligence) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** the page describes network awareness, situational awareness with 3D visualization, shift support, emergency support, event automation, mobile mine-status awareness and customizable dashboards. **Documented interface pattern:** a progression from communications coverage to shared awareness, shift/emergency support, and integrated-data review; no detailed UI behavior was documented. **MineGuard should learn:** make communications coverage and visibility an explicit precursor to operational interpretation and action; tailor information to users. **Do differently:** never imply guaranteed evacuation, complete personnel accounting or verified real-time coverage.

### Hexagon Mine Safety

**Official source:** [Hexagon Mine Safety](https://hexagon.com/products/mine-safety) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** portfolio overview mentions collision avoidance, alertness monitoring, personal protection, proximity/fatigue/distraction monitoring and intervention products. It describes separate operator and manager information needs, but some statements are portfolio-wide and do not establish every function for every product. **Documented interface pattern:** page organizes material around detection, intervention and insights, followed by named products; no operational UI verified. **MineGuard should learn:** distinguish observation, intervention and management insights, with role-appropriate context. **Do differently:** do not claim MineGuard detection, machine intervention or product compatibility and do not generalize portfolio claims to each product.

### OPC Foundation OPC UA for Mining, OPC-40560

**Official source:** [OPC-40560 scope](https://reference.opcfoundation.org/specs/OPC-40560/1) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** defines information models/types and use-case information exchanges for mining machines, equipment, systems and services, including machine-to-machine and machine-to-superordinate-system communication. The source says use cases may be extended for manufacturer/operator needs. **Documented interface pattern:** none; it describes information models, not screens. **MineGuard should learn:** make source, field meaning, machine/system type and use-case context inspectable. **Do differently:** present OPC UA as an interoperability target only; no MineGuard conformance, safety certification or validated threshold follows from citing an information-model specification.

### W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2

**Official source:** [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and [W3C accessibility standards overview](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** WCAG organizes testable criteria around perceivable, operable, understandable and robust content. It covers keyboard operation, semantic structure/relationships, component name/role/state, contrast and use of color. **Documented patterns:** WCAG criteria are normative requirements for accessibility at stated levels; do not treat this as mine-safety research. **MineGuard should learn:** use keyboard-operable controls, programmatic labels and state, text alternatives, sufficient contrast and non-color status cues. **Do differently:** do not claim conformance from adopting selected patterns; no accessibility audit was performed.

### Caterpillar / Cat Mining

**Official source:** [Caterpillar Mining](https://www.cat.com/en_US/by-industry/mining.html) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** high-level page presents equipment, technology, services and people as a combined mining offer and names Cat MineStar; underground-specific functions, modules, integrations and safety workflows were not established in the reviewed text. **Documented interface pattern:** named categories and calls to explore further material, not a MineStar operational dashboard. **MineGuard should learn:** keep the broader operational setting legible while clearly separating underground-specific functions. **Do differently:** do not infer safety features, technical specs, integration or performance from a broad marketing overview.

### ABB mining automation and digital applications

**Official sources:** [ABB mining automation](https://new.abb.com/mining/mining-automation) and [ABB mining](https://new.abb.com/mining) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** ABB describes control-system, digital-application and remote-operations offerings, and recommendation workflows where people sign off on suggested actions. **Documented interface pattern:** category/product descriptions on public pages; no operational decision-support screen was verified. **MineGuard should learn:** distinguish observed data from recommendations and keep consequential suggestions reviewable by a person. **Do differently:** do not imply ABB endorsement, integration, equivalent performance, an autonomous authority or substitution for mine control/ventilation systems.

### Becker Mining Systems / MineView AI

**Official source:** [Becker MineView AI](https://www.becker-mining.com/en/mineviewai) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** vendor describes visual analytics for mining, including drill/conveyor monitoring and PPE detection, live alerts and a dashboard with live video and history; it describes pilot comparison to site ground truth, without published measured accuracy in the reviewed page. **Documented interface pattern:** topic anchor index and textual description of a combined live/history dashboard; no dashboard screenshot verified. **MineGuard should learn:** pair events with historical context, make tasks explicit and recognize that site suitability and ground-truth validation matter. **Do differently:** do not promise accuracy, compatibility, safety outcomes, fail-safe behavior or regulatory compliance from product marketing statements.

### Exyn Technologies

**Official source:** [Exyn underground mine cavity monitoring](https://www.exyn.com/industries/mining-cavity-monitoring) — retrieved 2026-11-04. The user-supplied `/mining` URL did not resolve to a working product page; the official navigation led to this page. **Relevant functionality:** page describes pilot-free drone/portable ground systems, 3D maps, sensor data, georeferenced models, Exyn Nexys and underground cavity monitoring. **Documented interface pattern:** descriptive headings and links to demos/case studies; no dashboard UX documented. **MineGuard should learn:** separate acquisition, mapping and interpretation stages; display map evidence with status and provenance. **Do differently:** MineGuard is not a drone, mapping system or autonomous controller; vendor claims are not independent performance evidence.

### Emesent Hovermap

**Official source:** [Emesent mining](https://www.emesent.com/industry/mining/) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** vendor describes autonomous mapping across mine environments, point-cloud capture, repeat surveys and workflows for tasks including volumetrics, over/under-break, convergence and inspection. **Documented interface pattern:** named use cases and linked case studies on the website, not a decision-support UI. **MineGuard should learn:** connect repeat observations to change-over-time views and keep captured scan evidence distinct from risk interpretation. **Do differently:** no MineGuard scanning capability or performance follows from Emesent’s product claims; do not imply compatibility or regulatory compliance.

### U.S. Mine Safety and Health Administration (MSHA)

**Official sources:** [MSHA data sources and calculators](https://www.msha.gov/data-and-reports/data-sources-and-calculators), [MSHA about](https://www.msha.gov/about), [MSHA safety and training](https://www.msha.gov/safety-and-training) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** MSHA pages describe the agency’s enforcement/assistance role and public information about mines, inspections, accidents, injuries, violations, statistics, training and mine data. The Mine Data Retrieval System is described as collecting current/historical data; the tool UI itself was unavailable for inspection. **Documented interface pattern:** textual page groups for mine data, reports, statistics, training and resources; no dynamic UI behavior verified. **MineGuard should learn:** separate operational records, reports, training and trend summaries; identify data source/timeframe and stable mine identifiers. **Do differently:** MineGuard is not an MSHA service; outputs are not official compliance determinations, reporting or legal advice.

### CDC / NIOSH Mining Program

**Official sources:** [NIOSH machinery struck-by injuries and proximity controls](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/mining/topics/machinery-struck-by-injuries.html), [NIOSH Mining tools](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/mining/tools/) and [Mining data and statistics](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/mining/data/default.html) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** research on mining hazards/proximity systems and downloadable tools; the data page describes selectable charts, maps and tables based in part on MSHA data, not a real-time sensor feed. **Documented interface pattern:** topics grouped by hazard/prevention and reportable chart/map/table outputs. **MineGuard should learn:** relate risk indicators to their evidence and make history and source context inspectable. **Do differently:** do not present research or historical statistics as MineGuard validation, live underground data, regulatory approval or guaranteed prevention.

### CSA Group Mining Safety standards catalog

**Official sources:** [CSA Mining Safety archive](https://www.csagroup.org/store/product-list/worker-public-safety/a0K1I000003kz8xUAA/) and [CSA standards overview](https://www.csagroup.org/standards/) — retrieved 2026-11-04. **Relevant functionality:** catalog lists mining-related standards/training by designation/title, year, language and format; exact requirements and applicability depend on the relevant edition and jurisdiction. **Documented interface pattern:** year-filtered list and detail pages with scope/edition distinctions. **MineGuard should learn:** show exact designation, edition and scope whenever a standard is referenced. **Do differently:** do not convert excerpts into legal advice, MineGuard compliance status or instructions for untrained people. Verify the applicable official text and rely on qualified review.

## Cross-source UX synthesis

The research supports an interface organized by operational questions, not a single undifferentiated list of features. Users should be able to move from a location or alert to the related record, inspect the observations behind a classification, and distinguish recorded evidence from calculations, analysis and recommended action. Spatial views benefit from complementary list-based alternatives and explicit communication/data-health status. Role-specific landing views reduce unnecessary detail. Trend/history context should not obscure whether data are current, complete or merely sample records. Accessibility is a parallel interaction quality: keyboard operation, semantics, contrast and non-color indicators matter, but accessibility does not validate mine-safety decisions.

MineGuard's defining difference is to state what it knows, what it does not know and why. Every demonstration item is labelled simulated or otherwise clearly sourced; a silent sensor is a loss of information rather than a safe reading. The software supports a professional's review, never replacing inspections, certified instruments, qualified judgement, site procedures or approved emergency response. Vendor statements remain attributed and their missing technical details remain unavailable.

## Role and task strategy

| Role | Primary question | Default workspace | Critical information and workflow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mine Safety Officer | What needs verification today? | Hazards & Inspections | Prioritized inspection queue, reason/evidence, last inspection, information gaps; create, assign, track and complete a task. |
| Mine Manager | What is unresolved and who is accounted for? | Command Centre summary | Open alerts, unresolved zones, workforce counts, data freshness and shift changes; assign attention and review handover. |
| Mining Engineer / Surveyor | What does the spatial data show, and where are the gaps? | Mine Map / scan comparison | Zone state, scan coverage, sample differences, provenance and missing areas; compare records and open a zone. |
| Control Room Operator | What is alarming versus silent right now? | Live Monitoring | Measurement thresholds, stale/silent communication, timestamps, alert queue; acknowledge, inspect source and escalate through demo workflow. |
| Procurement / Engineering | Which technology category might fit this problem? | Technology & Vendors | Verified vendor facts, sources, missing information, comparison and limitations; use an information-gathering advisor, not a purchase recommendation. |

## Prototype limits that stay visible

No data are connected. Sensor thresholds and scores are illustrative, configurable demo rules—not statutory limits or prediction of collapse. Zone data and scan geometry are fictional. Worker records are pseudonymous and are not live locations. Copilot examples are not regulatory advice. Vendor directory facts are only claims found on official product pages and carry source attribution. MineGuard has no real OPC UA adapter or mine integration in this demo. A source being official does not prove vendor performance or MineGuard compliance.
