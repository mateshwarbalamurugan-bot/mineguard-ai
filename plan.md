## Implementation and design

- **App stack:** client-side React + TypeScript + Vite, on the initialized project's configured preview port. The prototype uses no server, managed database, third-party safety service or real-world data connection. A single versioned Zustand store persists in localStorage and resets exactly to seed. A static route manifest is served from `/manus-routes.json` before the first dev-server start.
- **Project structure:** `src/components` common shell, menus, drawers, badges, tables, modals, alerts and accessible charts; `src/pages` the workspaces; `src/data` seed and the 13 research records; `src/models` domain types/selectors; `src/rules` transparent confidence/priority scoring; `src/simulation` scenario events, cascade and replay; `src/vendors` directory, advisor and radar; `src/copilot` sample answers; `src/store` versioned persistence/actions. `public` contains local app assets, manifest, service worker and route manifest. `docs/research-ux.md` is the internal research deliverable.
- **Visual language:** use the user's control-room palette and hierarchy: navy `#0B1220` sidebar, `#F6F8FB` workspace, teal `#0891B2`, Known green `#16A34A`, Uncertain amber `#D97706`, Unknown slate `#94A3B8` with a diagonal hatch and Alert red `#DC2626`. Use Inter for UI and JetBrains Mono for measurements, IDs and times; body copy is at least 14px. A custom, restrained MineGuard tunnel/trace symbol provides product identity. No decorative stock images.
- **Navigation and user modes:** Command Centre; Monitoring & Mine Map with Live Monitoring, 2D/3D Map and Scans; Hazards & Inspections with Knowledge Confidence and Inspection Priority; Workforce & Emergency; Mining Copilot; Technology & Vendors with Directory, Advisor and Radar; Reports; Integrations & Settings; with Source Register and About/Roadmap in secondary navigation. Role-switcher defaults: Safety Officer → Hazards & Inspections; Mine Manager → Command Centre summary; Engineer/Surveyor → Map scan comparison; Control Room Operator → Monitoring; Procurement/Engineering → Technology & Vendors.
- **Traceable state:** actions are persisted and audited. The derivation chain is scenario/event → sensor state and timestamps → freshness → Knowledge Confidence Engine → zone classification → alert generation → inspection priority → map, KPIs, report and timeline. Tunnel B sensor failure must drive the entire chain without reload. Unknown/missing inputs never mean safe. Acknowledgement workflow is new → acknowledged → linked to task → resolved.
- **Offline and visualizations:** keep the sample demo entirely local, PWA-ready, and usable without a network. Use an accessible 2D schematic and an illustrative interactive 3D tunnel model, explicitly labelled “Illustrative demonstration model – not real LiDAR.” Scan differences use sample geometry, not real LiDAR. Map/text alternatives and data provenance accompany the visuals.
- **Vendor Directory:** only the eight vendors and corrected source-supported scope in the revised brief: Sandvik, Epiroc, Hexagon, Caterpillar, ABB, Becker Mining Systems, Exyn Technologies and Emesent. Tag source-derived fields `VENDOR-PROVIDED`; unavailable fields say `Not available`. No field is `INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED`. All vendors carry: “Vendor-provided descriptions. Not independent verification of performance.” Compatibility notes, integration information, certifications, pricing and technical specifications are `Not available` unless the research base explicitly states otherwise. In particular, Caterpillar underground functions are `Not available`; the invalid Exyn `/mining` URL is not used.
- **Copilot and integrations:** keep every answer labelled `SAMPLE RESPONSE – source to be verified`, with source title/URL and section `not specified in reviewed page` for relevant MSHA, NIOSH or CSA page-level citations; jurisdiction labels are MSHA/NIOSH = United States and CSA = Canada/standards body. - **Accessibility and motion:** semantic landmarks, keyboard operation and visible focus, correctly named controls, text summaries for charts/maps, `aria-live` alert announcements, non-color status encodings and a reduced-motion setting. Follow the requested motion system (data flow, sensor heartbeat, zone transition, red alert, cascade, priority reorder, map focus, scan sweep, worker checkpoint replay, emergency transition, guided spotlight and micro-interactions) and suppress movement under reduced-motion preference.
